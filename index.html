FICTIONAL EVIDENCE DESK

Can you trust this claim?

Scrutiny is not automatic distrust. Check the graph, the sample, and what the data can actually support.

Sampling bias: some parts of a population are more likely to enter a sample.

Correlation: two variables vary together; by itself, that does not establish cause.

10–15 minutes. All organisations, headlines and datasets in the lesson are fictional and synthetic.

FRESH HEADLINE CHECK

“Focus pods boosted scores by 40%!” — fictional claim

The synthetic data actually show 70 to 74 points. The graphic starts at 68, and the sample came only from volunteers who already used the pods.

NOTEBOOK SUMMARY

Ask what the evidence supports.

  • Axis choices can change visual emphasis without changing numbers.
  • Sample method matters; more biased observations do not automatically remove bias.
  • Association can be real while a causal claim remains unsupported.

Next practice: for any chart, write one observation, one limitation and one conclusion no stronger than the evidence.
